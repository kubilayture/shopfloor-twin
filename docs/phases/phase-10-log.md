### P10-F2 / #295

- #295 (P10-F2, branch `fix/295-spc-number-format`): numbers and times on the web as people read them. Display only; no stored value, API payload or contract changed.
  - **Precision rule (decision):** the characteristic carries its own precision in the contract: `resolution` (from `config/plant/process.yaml`'s leak test, `0.001` Pa/min; `quality.yaml` itself names none), and every SPC value comes with its exact `steps`. So: readings and ranges of readings (I, MR and R points, retests) are shown with the resolution's decimals (3 for the leak test); anything averaged or estimated (X̄ points, centre lines, control limits) with one more (4), the usual SPC practice of one digit beyond the data; Cpk keeps two. With subgroups of 5 an X̄ is a whole number of 0.0002 steps, so four decimals are exact; if the subgroup size becomes configurable, an X̄ with n = 3 or 4 is rounded at that one extra decimal (not exact). A value that is no finite number (NaN or ±Infinity in a payload) is shown as `–`. An SPC value is rounded from its exact steps × resolution, so the shown digits are the MES's exact value, not the double. Where the payload names no resolution (the serial trace's reading, which carries value, unit and limit only; a telemetry value; the specification limit), at most six decimals with trailing zeros dropped: it strips the binary noise and never hides an instrument step coarser than 10⁻⁶ (process.yaml's resolutions are 0.1 to 0.001). Rounding is half away from zero on the exact decimal (`oee-core`'s `toDecimal`), never `Number.toFixed` (`1.005.toFixed(2)` is `1.00`). Code: `apps/web/src/domain/numbers.ts`.
