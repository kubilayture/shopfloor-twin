- Interval finality per piece (#113; ADR-0018 amended): a stored state piece is final once it ends at or before its machine's "final through" (the latest time of its own device facts with no open sequence gap below them; a gap reported as an incident counts as closed) or its epoch is closed, so a completed shift bucket has `provisionalMs = 0` once its machine reported after it. New table `equipment.bucket_finality` (migration `0005_equipment_finality.sql`): per (equipment, epoch, UTC hour) when it became final, its revision (raised by a late fill after an incident or a late fact of a closed epoch) and its gap incidents; it survives restarts and is in `docs/data-model.md`. `shiftTotals` and `GET /api/v1/shifts/{date}/{shift}/totals` report `revision` and `incidents` per row (OpenAPI regenerated). Ingest's device fact notices carry `settledSeq`, sweeps send a `gaps` notice, and `Ingest.settled` returns a device stream's gap state; a stream first seen above seq 1 holds finality back below it for the gap grace period, then writes that history off without an incident (a warning and `mes_ingest_history_written_off_total`). A jump in the settled part rechecks ingest's state-fact count, so a fact that never got a notice is recomputed before anything is final; a closed epoch's final hours get its `epoch-ended` incidents. The golden-week reconciliation checks every piece's flag, every final hour and every bucket's revision and incidents; the live reconciliation drops the "totals stable" wait, runs on until every machine has sent a fact after the shift end (`RECONCILE_SILENT_WAIT_MS`, reported as `silentWait`) and requires shift A final with no revision and no incident.
