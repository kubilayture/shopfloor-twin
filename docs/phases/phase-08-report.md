## Summary

Every packed part can be traced back to what it is made of and how it was tested, every lot forward to where it went, and the leak test is under statistical control:

- **Facts for traceability** (#196, #197): the cycle facts carry the lots consumed for the part and, at ASM-01, the operator at the cycle's start, as fields of the existing OPC UA cycle event (address space 1.2) and UNS cycle message (contract 1.4), so no device sequence number and no earlier event log changed; ingest stores them with a lot index.
- **Pure packages** (#195): `spc-core` (I-MR and X̄-R in exact fractions, frozen limit versions, Nelson rules 1–8 with ties defined, signal episodes, the measured false-alarm run length of the defaults) and `trace-core` (`attachConsumption`, the packing rule, the trace tree, `traceAsOf`).
- **Ground truth** (#208): `trace.json` beside every golden summary, with the counterfactual readings of an injected leak drift; a new golden `quality-01` with small lots, a rework on a second seal-kit lot, a leak scrap and a +0.6 Pa/min drift detected two readings after its start.
- **ERP mock** (#198): idempotent quality notifications and a per-lot view.
- **`genealogy` module** (#199): lots from the ERP, serial units over Phase 7's units, lot links per operation and pass, cartons by the packing rule on final data, incidents, the contiguous processed watermark and trace queries that say whether they are final.
- **`quality` module** (#200, #201): SPC on first-pass leak readings with versioned limits, provisional and final episodes, acknowledgement; quality holds on a lot, serial, carton or order whose scope follows the genealogy, a close guard under one advisory lock, and notifications to the ERP.
- **Screens** (#202, #203): traceability search and serial, lot and carton pages; SPC charts with episodes; holds list, detail and opening; quality banners on the terminal.
- **End to end** (#204): a third live reconcile pass running `quality-01`'s shift A, Playwright SPC, trace and hold checks, and `pnpm test:trace-perf`.
- **Fixes along the way:** #192 (an `alarm-core` defect found by the Phase 7 closing gate), #212 and #216 (shared test ports and timing under parallel load), #218 (an exactly-once race in the MES's command settling, found by a stress run).

All seven exit criteria are met by local runs. The closing runs on this branch: `pnpm verify` passed uncached three times in a row with random property seeds (exit criterion 7's last clause, the one not recorded before); the full `pnpm gate` and `pnpm test:trace-perf`: both passed (`GATE-OK` and `TRACE-PERF-OK` on `27395dd`) (see "The closing gate").

## Defects found, and how each was fixed

Every pull request had an independent review (ADR-0011). As in Phases 5 to 7, the findings that mattered came from a reviewer running something: a probe against PostgreSQL, a crafted interleaving, a mutant of the code under test, or a stress run under load.

- **#192 (Phase 6 code, found by the Phase 7 closing gate), fixed in PR #206:** `alarm-core`'s `revisionSpans` started a superseded occurrence's span at the late raise, so the earlier hours it changed were not revised. The span now starts at the occurrence's own raise. The review also found the MES's own guard at the hour's end (`>` instead of `>=`), so a supersede or end exactly on the hour changed the previous final hour without a revision. The counterexample is reference case 23, and the property generator is biased toward hour and window boundaries; without the pinned example it finds #192 in 2 of 3 runs of 30,000.
- **Plan (PR #207), three rounds, before any code:** the close-guard race reproduced in probe P2 (a guard reading `hold_unit` closed an order beside an open hold); an SPC criterion a false alarm could satisfy (now counterfactual); no defined live-versus-golden comparison (now `traceAsOf` at 14:00 against a headless reference); the close guard reading a lagging genealogy (now the watermark and `genealogy-pending`); the lock order against the orders queue.
- **Pure packages (PR #210):** the first defaults gave about 50 false episodes a week, so rule 1 on MR was dropped (13 per 1,000, P(clean 60) 0.57, detection unchanged); rules 5 and 6 named a centre-line point as the signal; tautological property tests and unchecked expectation rows. The reviewer's 12 mutations were each caught after the fix.
- **Facts (PR #211, PR #214):** the golden lot check reused `attachConsumption`, so an operator taken at the cycle's end passed (now derived from the raw events, and the mutation fails); ingest refused the whole cycle over bad trace fields, a sequence gap over auxiliary data (now `trace-dropped`); the smoke depended on live production.
- **Ground truth (PR #213):** carton closing times and lot aggregates were checked only against shared code, and the live trace by hash only; now derived independently and cut on the server (`?asOf`).
- **Test ports and timing (#212, PR #215; #216, PR #217):** the recurring gateway, MES and web "flakes" under parallel load were fixed ports, shared certificate folders, readiness a foreign process could pass, Mosquitto reported running before Docker's port forward passed connections, a 300 ms Modbus timeout read as device silence, and fixed sleeps. Fixed in `test-kit` (runtime ports, own log line plus health, an MQTT CONNECT probe) and in the tests (count-based waits, server-side timings). The review's mutation test showed that the widened terminal bound let the #190 stale-memo bug pass; a clock-free test now pins `onChange → memoized.clear()` (fails 3 of 3 with the bug).
- **#218, the exactly-once race (PR #221), found by a stress run:** under load, `orders.e2e` raised `duplicate-job` once in eight runs. `Orders.#evaluate` read job facts before the CNC's progress; an acceptance committed between the two reads was counted as received but missing from the facts, so a command was settled `not-accepted` while its job existed (in production, a second job needs the race twice). The module audit found no other "facts, then progress" read. ## Process notes

- **Phase log kept from the first merge** (the Phase 7 lesson): `docs/phases/phase-08-log.md` has an entry for every merged PR and every review round, and the wave A checkpoint (plan "Phase size and waves") was recorded before wave B started on `9bbd5e0`.

That load turned latent defects into visible failures: the shared test ports (#212, #216), the reconciliation timeouts and the #218 race. Load is now part of how a fix is shown: stress runs under one `yes` per core, beside a looping e2e suite, are recorded in the log for each.
- **Two disk-full incidents** (coordinator-reported; no PR body or log entry records them): twice the Colima VM's disk filled while heavy suites and Testcontainers ran in parallel, which caused I/O errors in containerd's `meta.db`. Since then long runs check free disk first (`df -h /`), and the gate removes its Compose project, volumes, images and env file; the closing runs of this report did the same (free disk before and after in "The closing gate").
- **The containerd store** (coordinator-reported): the `meta.db` I/O errors above were the corruption, repaired by the same `colima restart`. No result in this report comes from a run during either incident.

## Known issues and carry-overs

- **GitHub Actions is still disabled** by the spending limit; merges are gated locally, and the updated workflow is untested in Actions.
- **Known limits from the ADRs:**
  - ADR-0032: with the defaults a stable process opens about 13 episodes per 1,000 readings, about 29 a week, which quality acknowledges; the module re-derives the whole chart on every reading, fine for a week (p95 13–14 ms) but **a month of readings (about 9,000) needs incremental derivation or a window, a Phase 9 item with retention**; **a late reading that moves every later point costs 23–25 ms idle and 30–45 ms loaded, over the 20 ms budget** (rare: a gap filled late);

## Lessons

- **Ask what a missing fact would mean before choosing a watermark.** The command path settles on `receivedThrough` because a lost fact might be the acceptance; genealogy uses `settledSeq` because it must move past a lost fact. The same word, "covered", needed a second check (`unrecorded`) once written-off numbers counted as settled. A watermark is a claim; state which claim.
- **Read what settles before what it judges, in every module.** #218 is the fourth instance of the Phase 5–7 defect class, now across two statements of one function. The audit after it found no other, and "progress first" is written into ADR-0029.
- **A detector is tested by its counterfactual.** With 13 false episodes per 1,000 readings, "SPC signalled after the drift" passes by chance one time in five. Recording the same draws without the drift and requiring silence there made the criterion mean what it says.
- **Measure the defaults before writing them down.** The plan's rule set came from reasoning; the measured run length showed it would raise about 50 false episodes a week and that MR rule 1 caught nothing. The ADR now carries the numbers.
- **Finality must be under test, not just correct in order.** A replay that is either perfectly ordered or fully shuffled never puts a final/provisional boundary under test. Bounded disorder found what two mutants had hidden.
- **Mutation is the review's sharpest tool.** At least five PRs of this phase (#214, #215, #220, #222, #225) had a test that passed with its behaviour removed; each was found by a reviewer breaking the code on purpose. A fake-MES harness that feeds wrong answers did the same for the end-to-end comparisons.
- **Under parallel agents, the machine is shared state.** Fixed ports, fixed folders and fixed sleeps worked for one run at a time and failed beside four. Runtime ports, per-suite folders and readiness from the child's own output are the default now, and stress under load is part of showing a fix.
- **Keep every check that can't run in the gate in its own record.** `pnpm test:trace-perf` and the three uncached `verify` runs are not gate steps; without the closing records they would have had no commit attached.
- **Disk is a gate resource.** Two runs were lost to a full disk. Check `df` before a gate, and let the gate remove what it built.
