- #212 (branch `fix/212-gateway-test-ports`): the gateway suites no longer flake when other runs share the machine. Root causes found: fixed simulator ports (collector control 7900, poller control 8000, executor proxies 8291–8295 …), shared with every parallel worktree or agent, and a fixed client certificate directory the collector test wiped at start; a readiness check that a foreign process on the same port could pass; Mosquitto's "running" log line arriving before Docker's port forward passed connections on, so a subscriber without reconnects hung (the outbox's re-subscribe timeouts); a 300 ms Modbus timeout with `silentAfter: 2`, so two slow answers on a loaded machine read as the injected silence (the extra `device-silent`/`device-recovered`); fixed sleeps that assumed the collectors and the poller had caught up; and a race in the outbox broker-outage test, where the gateway (or MQTT.js's re-send) could reach the restarted broker before the subscriber had subscribed. Fixed in `@shopfloor/test-kit` (runtime ports with a checked OPC UA block, ready = own log line plus health, retry on early exit, children killed on exit, an MQTT CONNECT probe in `startBroker`, `TcpProxy.retarget`, `timed`) and in the tests (count-based waits, a 2 s Modbus timeout, the second alarm snapshot checked by replay, the outbox's broker behind a proxy held down until the subscriber is back, "closes promptly" within 2 s plus measured lag). Measured: `pnpm --filter @shopfloor/edge-gateway test` 10 of 10 while `pnpm --filter @shopfloor/mes-server test:e2e` ran alongside (4 of 4 e2e passes, another agent's suites also running), and 10 of 10 under 8 × `yes` on 8 cores; before the broker probe, the first run alongside the e2e failed on "subscribing to the restarted broker".

- #218 (branch `fix/218-duplicate-job-race`): the `duplicate-job` seen under load in `orders.e2e` ("ends exactly once…", `PO-M12~OP10~1`) was a real MES race, not the test's injected answer. `PO-M12` is round 1 of case (b); its injected `unknown` (`seqAfterExpiry` 9 = `held`) was consistent with the device: the acceptance had number 9 (`stored_seq` 8, `seen` [[2, 8]] before). Captured timeline of the failing run (one of 8 under one `yes` per core, before the fix): delivery 17:53:53.471Z; acceptance fact 9 with the gateway's `receivedAt` 53.544, then held in its outbox while the broker was cut; injected `unknown` stored at 59.730; fact 9 stored at 17:54:01.602 (with its cursor, `seen` [[2, 9]]); `duplicate-job` raised at 01.781 ("settled without a job (not-accepted)"). `#evaluate` read the job facts (none) and only then each CNC's `deviceProgress`; fact 9 was committed between the two, so `receivedFrom` (the `seen` range holding `stored_seq` 8) reached 9 while the facts lacked it: `not-accepted`, persisted, an acceptance with the incident one tick later. A scripted interleaving in `orders.test.ts` (an ingest proxy that stores the acceptance inside `deviceProgress`) fails without the fix and passes with it. Learned: a pure reading over several stores is only as consistent as the order of its reads; read what settles before what it is checked against.
