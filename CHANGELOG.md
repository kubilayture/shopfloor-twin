## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Downtime coding, the MES's first writes (#121, ADR-0023, ADR-0021 amended; migration `0009`).
  - **Codings** (`equipment.downtime_coding`): time ranges inside a record's loss time with an active reason (not UNCODED), a comment and `coded_by` (free text, unauthenticated), stored by time so they survive a re-cut. A split is two codings; uncoded time keeps the auto reason. `equipment.downtime_coding_set` holds the optimistic-concurrency version per (equipment, epoch).
  - **Re-cuts.** After `piecesChanged` (and once at start-up) codings are clipped to the loss time they still cover; the rest is kept as `orphaned`, audited and raises the version.
  - **Audit** (`equipment.downtime_audit`): action, before, after, actor, wall time, request id, version; append-only by a trigger (UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE refused).
  - **Reasons in effect.** Records carry `reason` (most of their time), `segments`, `codings`, `uncoded` and `version`; `uncoded=true` and the UNCODED-first order count uncovered time; the Pareto sums coded time under the coding's reason. OEE never reads codings: a recode or split leaves A, P, Q and every loss identical (tested).
  - **API.** `PUT /api/v1/downtime/{id}/coding` (`{segments, codedBy, version}`; 409 on a stale version, 422 outside the record, overlapping or an uncodable reason) and `GET /api/v1/downtime/{id}/audit`; schemas in `@shopfloor/contracts/mes-api`; OpenAPI regenerated. Push: `recoded` on the `downtime` topic when codings change.
  - **Write guards** on every non-GET request under `/api/v1`: allowed `Host`; `MES_WRITES=off` → 405; `Origin` required, same host and port (not scheme) or allowlisted, else 403; `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin` when sent; `application/json` or 415; 16 KiB bodies (413); a global write rate limit (10/s, bursts of 20; 429); no CORS headers; `X-Request-Id` on every write. `MES_WRITES` in `env.ts` and Compose.
  - **Proxies.** nginx passes only GET, POST, PUT and DELETE under `/api/` and caps bodies at 16 KiB; the Vite dev and preview proxy rewrites `Origin` on HTTP requests by its loopback-only rule, so writes work from `pnpm dev`.
  - **Stack smoke** checks the 403 (foreign and missing `Origin`), 415 (form) and nginx's method refusals; with `SMOKE_WRITES=1` (CI) it also codes one closed, final, uncoded record through nginx with the page's origin, clears it and checks the audit.
  - The MES refuses `HTTP_ALLOWED_HOSTS=*` while `MES_WRITES=on`; only exact origin strings count as same-origin; a write reads its record `FOR SHARE`, so a concurrent re-cut is clipped after it; failed clips back off exponentially (at most 30 s).
- `oee` module and read API (#120, ADR-0021, ADR-0022).
  - **OEE on read.** `apps/mes-server/src/modules/oee` owns no tables. It reads, through the other modules' `index.ts`, the hourly state rows and the Stopped pieces with their whole interval's length in one REPEATABLE READ snapshot (`Equipment.oeeInputs`; the Stopped time is derived from those pieces), and the cycles from `cycle_hourly`. Per equipment and per work center (sums of the machines' inputs), per UTC hour and per shift bucket (the hours grouped by shift), per device epoch (newest by default). A bucket is `final` when every hour is final in equipment, both aggregates are refreshed through it, no open stop is still below the threshold and every member is complete; it carries `provisionalMs`, `revision` and `incidents`. An open stop's whole length runs to the later of the machine's "final through" and its stored end.
  - **REST under `/api/v1`.** `GET /oee`, `/shifts/{date}/{shift}/oee`, `/shifts`, `/shifts/current`, `/reason-codes`, `/downtime` and `/downtime/pareto`, zod-validated with bounded ranges and problem details; response schemas in `@shopfloor/contracts/mes-api` (results in the ground truth's JSON shape); OpenAPI regenerated.
  - **WebSocket.** Topic `oee:<equipment or work center>` (snapshot, then at most one delta per topic per second, computed off ingest's path) and `downtime` (records created, closed, recoded, updated, removed, from a debounced read after each `piecesChanged`); per-client limit of 16 OEE topics; `docs/api/realtime.md` and the message schema regenerated.
  - **Golden-week OEE reconciliation** (exit criterion 2): for every machine and both work centers in every shift bucket of the golden week, the MES's OEE equals the ground truth exactly (inputs, fractions, four-decimal strings, totals, losses, `unplanned`), in order and shuffled with 10 % duplicates, with no incident or revision; scheduled buckets are final, the no-shift bucket's OEE is null on both sides. Σ hours = shift for the OEE totals, the stop pieces per hour equal the ground truth's, and each shift's downtime Pareto equals its availability and micro-stop losses.
  - **Live reconciliation.** `pnpm test:reconcile` also requires shift A's `GET /api/v1/shifts/2026-10-12/A/oee` final for all six machines and equal to the live ground truth's OEE exactly, per machine and work center.

- Hourly continuous aggregates for OEE (#118, ADR-0022; migration `0006`): `equipment.state_hourly` (time and provisional time per state per equipment, epoch, UTC hour and shift bucket) and `ingest.cycle_hourly` (cycles per equipment, epoch, UTC hour, product and result), real-time, no refresh policies. Each owning module refreshes its aggregate explicitly through a shared helper (`AggregateRefresher`): every write below the watermark marks its hour dirty, a debounced (1 s) refresh off ingest's acknowledgement path covers `[earliest dirty hour, latest final hour + 1 h)` on its own connection, and a catch-up refresh runs at start-up. Equipment emits `piecesChanged`; ingest sends `cycle` and `epoch` notices. `Equipment.stateHourly` (with `final` and `revision`) and `Ingest.cycleHourly`/`cyclesRefreshed` are the read side for #120. `equipment.bucket_finality.cycles` remembers each final hour's cycle count; start-up compares it with `cycle_hourly` and revises an hour whose count changed unheard (the #113 proposal). The data-model generator documents views and continuous aggregates (definition, bucket, `materialized_only`), with drift tests.
- Reason codes, auto-classification and downtime records (#119, ADR-0021 amended).
  - **Cause vocabulary.** All causes are defined in `@shopfloor/contracts`: `CAUSES`, `materialCause`, `changeoverCause`, `NO_PRODUCT` and `causeVocabulary`. The simulator and the Modbus cause table use it. The golden week is byte-identical.
  - **Reason tree and rules.** `config/plant/reasons.yaml` holds the reason tree (ids are paths such as `BREAKDOWN/SPINDLE-OVERLOAD`) and an ordered list of rules from (state, cause pattern: exact, `{prefix}`, `null` or `{any}`) to a reason. The first match wins, and no match gives UNCODED.
  - **plant-config.** `plant-config` loads `reasons.yaml` outside the simulator's config hash and exports `classifyCause`. Its cross-check refuses:
    - unknown reasons;
    - parents not listed before their children;
    - a missing UNCODED;
    - loss states without a `null` fallback;
    - any cause of the plant model left UNCODED.
  - **Reason codes in master data.** Migration `0007_master_data_reason_codes.sql` adds `master_data.reason_code`. A reason the configuration drops stays, marked inactive. `MasterData.reasonCodes()` lists the tree.
  - **Downtime records.** Migration `0008_equipment_downtime_records.sql` adds `equipment.downtime_record` and `interval_source.downtime_threshold_ms`.
    - There is one record per availability-loss interval per scheduled shift bucket, keyed by its start in the bucket.
    - A Stopped interval makes a record from the micro-stop threshold on. Micro-stops, planned states and `no-shift` make none.
    - Records are stored in the same statement as the pieces on every interval path, and their `provisional` flag follows the pieces' finality.
    - Catch-up derives the records again when the threshold changed. Start-up recodes the records whose reason changed with the rules.
  - **Interface for #120.** `Equipment.downtimeRecords()` returns the records, with an UNCODED-first order. `availabilityLoss` is in `oee-core`.
  - **Tests.** A simulator test checks that every (state, cause) pair of the golden week is in the vocabulary and none is UNCODED. The golden-week reconciliation checks that every availability-loss interval has its records, that none is UNCODED, and that shuffled delivery stores the same records. `docs/data-model.md` is regenerated, and `docs/simulation-model.md` gains a "Causes" section.
- Simulator ground-truth OEE with `oee-core` (#117): `GroundTruth` keeps, per machine, the OEE inputs of every shift bucket and every UTC hour (time per state; Stopped intervals held until they close, then filed as pieces with their whole length; cycles per product and result, bucketed by their time with `shiftKeyAt`), and returns `bucketOee` per machine and shift bucket and `workCenterOee` for Cell M1 and Line A1, with fractions as `num/den` and four-decimal strings, losses, `unplanned`, `anomaly` and `provisional`. An open stop's whole length runs to the snapshot time (ADR-0021). Summary format `sim-summary/2` (new `oee` section; all other fields unchanged). Simulator control API 1.2: `GET /api/v1/ground-truth` includes the `oee` section; OpenAPI `info.version` `1.2.0`. Golden `week-01/summary.json` regenerated for the new section only (39,404 lines added; the schema line is the only changed line); `events.sha256` unchanged (`e7c69736…009b740b`), so the simulation itself is unchanged. A snapshot costs about 0.2 ms after a shift and about 3 ms after a week (response about 450 KB after a week).

### Added

- `oee-core` OEE (#116, ADR-0021): availability, performance, quality and OEE per machine bucket (`bucketOee`) and per work center from summed inputs (`workCenterOee`), with own and external availability loss, micro-stops, speed loss (may be negative) and quality loss; reduced BigInt fractions and half-up four-decimal rounding in integers (`fraction`, `toDecimal`); `null` where a ratio is undefined and an `anomaly` flag for counts without APT; open stops counted as provisional micro-stops. An ESLint rule forbids floating point in `oee-core`. `config/plant/oee.yaml` (micro-stop threshold 120 000 ms over the whole Stopped interval, `scheduledTime: shift-calendar`), loaded and validated by `plant-config`; `idealCycleMs` gives exact ideal times. `null` losses and `unplanned` counts for cycles when PBT = 0. `docs/oee-reference-cases.md`: 19 hand-calculated cases that the tests parse and check as fractions and four-decimal values; property tests for bounds, A·P·Q, loss accounting, hours summing to the shift and monotone rounding.
- Interval finality per piece (#113; ADR-0018 amended): a stored state piece is final once it ends at or before its machine's "final through" (the latest time of its own device facts with no open sequence gap below them; a gap reported as an incident counts as closed) or its epoch is closed, so a completed shift bucket has `provisionalMs = 0` once its machine reported after it. New table `equipment.bucket_finality` (migration `0005_equipment_finality.sql`): per (equipment, epoch, UTC hour) when it became final, its revision (raised by a late fill after an incident or a late fact of a closed epoch) and its gap incidents; it survives restarts and is in `docs/data-model.md`. `shiftTotals` and `GET /api/v1/shifts/{date}/{shift}/totals` report `revision` and `incidents` per row (OpenAPI regenerated). Ingest's device fact notices carry `settledSeq`, sweeps send a `gaps` notice, and `Ingest.settled` returns a device stream's gap state; a stream first seen above seq 1 holds finality back below it for the gap grace period, then writes that history off without an incident (a warning and `mes_ingest_history_written_off_total`). A jump in the settled part rechecks ingest's state-fact count, so a fact that never got a notice is recomputed before anything is final; a closed epoch's final hours get its `epoch-ended` incidents. The golden-week reconciliation checks every piece's flag, every final hour and every bucket's revision and incidents; the live reconciliation drops the "totals stable" wait, runs on until every machine has sent a fact after the shift end (`RECONCILE_SILENT_WAIT_MS`, reported as `silentWait`) and requires shift A final with no revision and no incident.
