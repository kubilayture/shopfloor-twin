## Reading the figures

- **The live rate** is P1's measured 13.35 messages per second at 60×, counting every outcome of `mes_ingest_messages_total` over the window: 6,404 messages in 480 s. An earlier version of this file said "about 17/s". That figure divided P2's published count by 5 minutes, but the count spans the whole run.
- **Excluded deliveries in P1's window:** 0 for each reason. The anchors and snapshots arrive when the gateway starts, before the window, and a fault-free shift delivers no duplicate.
- **T2:** 15 simulated hours of `orders-01` came to 11,867 published messages. The drain lasted 11.3 s, with `mes_ingest_pending_messages` peaking at 47. The 100 ms slopes cluster tightly: the median is 987.7 msg/s and the mean 974.8; T2 is judged on the lower of the two (the coordinator's re-review).
- **P3's load is mostly telemetry.** The facts keep the golden pacing at 180×. Telemetry runs at three times the gateway's live cadence: each OPC UA variable every 333 ms, and each washer value every 83 ms.
- **Percentiles** are printed interpolated within their bucket, as `histogram_quantile` does. The targets are judged exactly, as the share of observations at or below an edge. The edges are 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 and 500 ms, then 1, 2.5, 5, 10, 30 and 60 s.
- **Prometheus's view** agrees with the direct readings: `histogram_quantile(0.95, sum by (le) (increase(mes_ingest_lag_seconds_bucket[480s])))` at the end of P1 gave 46.2 ms against the suite's 46.1 ms.
